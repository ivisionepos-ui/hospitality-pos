Privacy

When you order, we use your name, phone number and (for delivery) your address only to make, deliver and contact you about that order.

Your order details are kept on this website for 30 days so you can follow your order, then deleted. The shop keeps a record of orders on its till system, as it must for its accounts.

We do not sell or share your details, send marketing, or use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser remembers your basket and your details on this device only, so you do not have to type them again; clear your browser's site data to remove them.

To work out the delivery charge we send your postcode (only) to postcodes.io, a free UK postcode service.

Questions, or to ask what we hold about you or to delete it: phone or visit the shop.
